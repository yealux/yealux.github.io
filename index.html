Suicide Prevention Month.

Suicide Prevention Month is here because many people feel like they do not belong.

Many people feel like they don't matter or belong, alongside facing mental health issues and trauma.

Most people forget how many people and young children lose their lives because of mental issues, trauma, not belonging and various other factors.

Remember, no matter what anyone says, you matter way more than you would ever think of.

If you have any relatives, friends, family or person in mind that might need help, check-in on them, a simple action can prevent so much.

If you yourself are going through this, please call 988 or the suicide crisis helpline.
